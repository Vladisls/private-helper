=============================================

The files in this folder (ocr\ next to CabalHelper.exe) are not written by Cabal Helper. They are used, unchanged,
to read the Farm Tracker's stack counts in digits-only mode. Each keeps its own licence:

x86\tesseract50.dll               Apache License 2.0 - https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tesseract

                                  Mark Adler), libpng 1.6.37 (libpng License), libjpeg-turbo 2.1.4 (IJG License and

Redistributable (x64 or x86), which most Windows PCs with games already have.
